# Branded Promo | Custom Promotional Products & Branded Merchandise

**Website:** https://www.brandedpromo.com/

## About Branded Promo

Branded Promo is a US supplier of custom promotional products and branded merchandise for businesses, organizations, schools, nonprofits, and event planners. The company helps customers source personalized products featuring their logos, artwork, and brand messaging for marketing campaigns, corporate events, trade shows, employee programs, and customer giveaways.

With an extensive selection of customizable merchandise, Branded Promo supports businesses looking for practical branded products, bulk promotional items, and tailored merchandise for different audiences and occasions.

## Custom Promotional Products

Branded Promo offers a broad selection of promotional products and branded merchandise, including:

- **Promotional Drinkware:** Custom water bottles, travel tumblers, coffee mugs, and other branded drinkware.
- **Promotional Pens:** Custom logo pens and writing instruments for business promotions, conferences, and events.
- **Bags and Totes:** Branded shopping bags, drawstring bags, tote bags, and promotional carrying accessories.
- **Office and Desk Products:** Custom notebooks, memo pads, stationery, and useful desk accessories.
- **USB and Technology Accessories:** Branded technology products and electronic promotional giveaways.
- **Apparel and Uniforms:** Custom clothing and branded apparel for businesses, teams, and events.
- **Healthcare Promotional Products:** Customized merchandise for healthcare organizations and awareness campaigns.
- **Event Giveaways:** Promotional merchandise for conferences, trade shows, exhibitions, and corporate gatherings.

## Custom Branding and Artwork Assistance

Promotional products can be customized with business logos, artwork, and brand messaging. Available decoration methods depend on the individual product and may include printing, full-color decoration, embroidery, laser engraving, and other product-specific processes.

Branded Promo offers:

- Free virtual product mockups and design revisions.
- Artwork assistance and guidance on preparing files for production.
- Product-specific branding and decoration options.
- Dedicated account managers to assist with orders.
- Bulk pricing and quotation support.
- Rush and priority service options on eligible products.

Customers can review product mockups and artwork before approving production.

## Bulk Promotional Products and Business Orders

Businesses often require branded merchandise in larger quantities for trade shows, marketing campaigns, employee onboarding, corporate gifting, and promotional programs.
